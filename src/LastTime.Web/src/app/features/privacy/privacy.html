Privacy & Security

LastTime is a small personal app. Here is, in plain words, how it treats your information.

Privacy

  • LastTime stores the email address you use for your account.
  • Your password is stored securely, as a hash, never as the password itself.
  • You can store your own items and their history, including descriptions, dates, costs, providers and notes.
  • Your account's data is intended to stay private to your account.
  • Data is kept in a hosted PostgreSQL database.
  • Your email address is used for email verification and for password recovery.
  • We do not sell users' personal information.

Security

  • Do not share your LastTime password with anyone.
  • Do not share verification codes or password-reset codes.
  • LastTime will never ask you to give your password or a verification code to another person.
  • Only enter information you are comfortable storing in the application.
  • If you believe your account has been compromised, contact the app administrator.

Feedback

Using LastTime? I'd love your feedback. If something is confusing, missing, doesn't work as expected, or you have an idea that would make the app more useful, let me know.
